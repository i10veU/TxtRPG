# Mythology & Transcendence Research

TxtRPG 장기 세계관에서 인간이 영웅·전설·신화·초월체·성좌·신격으로 이어지는 과정을 설계하기 위한 비교 연구 인덱스다.

## Research Question

> 인간이 언제 더 이상 인간으로만 기억되지 않고, 문명을 대표하는 전설·신화·초월적 존재가 되는가?

각 문명의 자료를 다음 공통 프레임으로 비교한다.

`창세/우주관 → 인간의 기원 → 문명/왕조의 기원 → 영웅 서사 → 시련/희생 → 죽음·저승·귀환 → 불멸/신격화 → 사회·왕권·질서 → 종말/재창조`

게임 설계 후보:

`인간 → 경험 → 능력 → 숙련 → 업적 → 전설 → 신화 → 인정/숭배 → 권능/권위 → 초월`

## Cross-Cultural Transformation Axes

1. Divine ancestry
2. Human-to-divine transformation
3. Immortality
4. Underworld descent and return
5. Death and rebirth
6. Monster/chaos defeat
7. Civilization or city founding
8. Sacred kingship
9. Hero cult / ancestor cult
10. Posthumous apotheosis
11. Name and reputation as a form of immortality
12. Cyclical destruction and rebirth of worlds
13. Divine authority tied to political/cultural power
14. Knowledge, craft, or ritual as a source of transcendence
15. Sacrifice as a prerequisite for transformation

## Initial Evidence Notes

- Mesopotamian sources show strong links between patron-deity status and city/political power; the rise of Babylon corresponds with Marduk's elevation within the pantheon.
- Gilgamesh provides a model of a partly divine human confronting mortality and seeking immortality.
- Korean Dangun traditions combine heavenly descent, culture-bringing, transformation, sacred ancestry, state founding, long life, and eventual mountain-spirit status.
- Korean Jumong traditions combine sacred lineage, extraordinary birth, exceptional martial ability, exile, and state foundation.
- Princess Bari links heroic action to the underworld and restoration of life, with regional variation in her eventual divine role.
